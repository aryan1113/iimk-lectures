# E-Commerce Marketing through Nykaa (Class Handout, Prof. Ekta Srivastava)

## Key Concepts

- **Touch points**: every opportunity a customer has to see or hear about the brand, or to have an encounter or experience with it.
- **Customer decision journey (CDJ)**: need recognition, information search, evaluation of alternatives, purchase decision, post-purchase behaviour. The handout also shows the purchase funnel, the McKinsey consumer-journey model and AIDA/hierarchy of effects (funnel analogy); the diagrams were lost, leaving only titles. Outside the slides: AIDA = attention, interest, desire, action; McKinsey's model is a loop of initial consideration, active evaluation, moment of purchase and post-purchase experience.
- **Owned / earned / paid media** (grouping reconstructed from a flattened table):
  - *Owned*: brand-controlled channels: website, blog, newsletter, email, direct mail, online newsroom (the list also includes employee uniforms and logos on company vehicles).
  - *Earned*: exposure others create: journalistic coverage, reviews and testimonials, word of mouth, user-generated content, likes and retweets.
  - *Paid*: bought exposure: ads and commercials, sponsorship, pay-per-click, banner ads.
  YouTube, Facebook and Twitter appear in the list as social channels; their placement is uncertain.
- **Role of content**: *risk reduction* (reviews, blogs/vlogs, Beauty Book); *social proof* (community comments, expert endorsement); *aspiration activation* (editorial content).
- **Search results** show **paid links** (sponsored/search ads) and **organic links**. Search ads give digital visibility and target *intent*, reaching customers as they express interest, so higher ROI is possible. Contrast with display ads: the handout asks whether search is most likely to capture only existing demand (posed as a question).
- **Search engine marketing**: advertisers purchase **keywords**; ads display when a user's search matches. *Exact/standard match* is less expensive; *broad/advanced match* is wider. Fee goes to the search engine **on click**.
- **Ad auction**: advertisers compete for space; it decides which ads show and in what order (ad ranking). Drivers: **bid amount** (maximum paid per click) and **ad quality/quality score** (keyword relevance and popularity via links from other pages, landing page, ad extensions with extra information).
- **App strategy, repurchase**: replenishment reminders, relevant cross-selling, and reduced friction via saved preferences, order history, wishlist and easy reordering. **Retention**: *personalisation* (recommend from past purchases, browsing, stated preferences); *notifications* (push messages on offers, availability, replenishment); *loyalty benefits* (points, member perks, exclusive offers).
- **SoMoLo**: *social* (discovery and influence: creators, reviews, sharing); *mobile* (convenient discovery and purchase: mobile-first, app, notifications); *location* (geographically relevant targeting and offers). It is not three separate channels; the question is how they reinforce one another.
- **Strategic integration across the funnel**: (1) *discovery* (SEO, paid search; site visit); (2) *evaluation* (reviews, Beauty Book, blogs, videos; understanding and consideration); (3) *conversion* (product page, cart, checkout, offers; purchase); (4) *post-purchase and repeat* (order communication, notifications, recommendations, loyalty; retention and higher lifetime value). **Integration means coordinating channels around the customer and the marketing objective, not merely using many channels.**
- **Measurement by objective**: awareness (reach, impressions); consideration (product-page and review engagement, add-to-cart); conversion (conversion rate, cost per acquisition, ROAS); retention (repeat rate, frequency, CLV); advocacy (review volume and quality, referrals).

## Why This Matters

It ties channels to journey stages and objectives so spend is judged by the stage it moves and how it is measured.

## Relevance for Exams / Cases

Classify examples as owned, earned or paid; explain how search ads are bought and ranked; map Nykaa's tools to funnel stages and metrics; explain integration and SoMoLo.
